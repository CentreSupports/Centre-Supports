Centre Supports Expands Community Impact in 2026

By CS Times • 2026

Since its recent establishment, Centre Supports, a local community resource hub, has launched a forward effort to build a website consolidating local businesses and nonprofits in Centre County, PA.

Starting in 2026, the organization has implemented new initiatives to improve user experience and expand outreach.

“We support victims and survivors of sexual assault, dating, and domestic violence… we really are a safety net for people of all ages.” — Jennifer Pancek, Centre Safe

Through partnerships, the platform highlights organizations like Childspace and the Office of Aging, showcasing their impact on families, children, and seniors.

“...child-centered environment to be in…a lot of families don’t have the kinds of equipment and materials that we have here in a space where kids can be with peers and learn from peers more than they can when they’re at home.” — Kelly Carpenter, Childspace

Action is being taken on a county-wide level as well; the Centre County Office of Aging has increased their number of programs.

“Our goal is to keep elderly people at home as long as possible ...our services are based on supporting an individual of 60 years age or older; in most cases they surround trying to allow the older adult to maintain their living situation for as long as possible.” — Quentin Burchfield, Office of Aging

Centre Supports aims to highlight these initiatives through their Youtube channel.


The platform offers:

  • Emergency hotlines
  • Childcare services
  • Mental health & substance support
  • Job & financial resources
  • Housing & transportation help

Each category offers different services that are tailored to help those struggling with various issues. It gives any Centre County individual access to local resources, shedding light on both small nonprofits and county-wide organizations. Centre Supports managers aim to create a cohesive, effective environment for the exploration of resources in Centre County. Additionally, the site includes a feedback page, allowing residents to request the inclusion of additional sites.


As for improvements around the county, interviewees have described great progress. Director Pancek describes, “We just launched our annual $50,000 holiday campaign to raise $50,000 to use for…our services and clients during the year” (Centre Safe). “I recently got a grant for $2,000.00 worth of outdoor equipment…we’re gonna be getting some new, more expensive equipment that I can’t usually afford on my regular budget… I just recently started offering drop-in services as well, so people who don’t need a consistent spot but might need something like a babysitter once in a while…have a place to bring their child that is convenient,” adds Ms. Carpenter. Learn more about progress and changes in Centre County in our seasonal newsletter!

With continued growth, Centre Supports aims to strengthen partnerships and expand access across the county.

Stay tuned with CS Times for more updates.
