OUR MISSION

Centre Supports aims to create an easy-to-navigate, efficient, and trustworthy site for the public of Centre County PA to rely on for the exploration of local resources. Our managers, staff, and administration take pride in our user-friendly, efficient interface.

Our goal is to ensure no one in Centre County is left behind regarding community assistance.
