Childcare Services

Photo: Centre County Children and Youth Services, a county-wide aid program.

Business Spotlight Childcare Services:

Childspace

Contact: (814) 237-6429


Childspace provides personalized care for children of 18 months to 5 years old. Their programs include educational and recreational activities that meet diverse needs and promote development.

YMCA of Centre County

Contact: (814) 237-7717


The YMCA’s School Age Child Care (SACC) program serves as a nurturing place for children for after and before school. It allows children to grow and learn outside of school.

Centre County Youth Service Bureau

Contact: (814) 237-5731


The Youth Service Bureau helps ensure that children and their families have ample opportunities to reach their full potential through support services and personalized care.

Centre County United Way

Contact: (814) 238-8283


United Way works to give everyone access to quality education. They fund programs with proven outcomes that provide child care and preschool opportunities or literacy education.

The Pennsylvania Key

Contact: (844)-569-7253


The Pennsylvania Key works with partners and Centre County organizations to provide educational, professional, and administrative services to educators, supporting improvement in early learning experiences for young children.

Centre County Children & Youth Services

Contact: (814) 355-6755


Centre County Children and Youth Services is dedicated to ensuring a safe home environment that is free from abuse, neglect, and exploitation for children around Centre County.
