Housing Services

Photo: Local residential housing community in State College

Business Spotlight Housing Services:

Out of the Cold

Contact: 814-852-8864


Out of the Cold is an easy-to-access homeless shelter that serves homeless citizens of Centre County. Hundreds of people are served by the shelter every single year. The shelter provides free showers, laundry, 3 meals, guidance, and more!

Housing Transitions

Contact: (814) 237-5508


Housing Transitions is a nonprofit dedicated to providing rental assistance, case management, and financial advice to the homeless. They offer 23 different rental housing units for the public of Centre County.

Centre County Housing

Contact: (814) 863-6571


Centre County Housing provides resources and hotlines if housing becomes a major problem. They offer resources to community support, shelters, guides, and more! With the different variety of services that they display, people will be able to easily find a housing service that can personally help them be more stable.

Centre County Community Land Trust

Contact: (814) 571-1179


State College Community Land Trust (SCCLT) keeps housing affordable by owning the land but with the home owners owning the house. The house’s price is limited such that the price is affordable to the public of Centre County. So far they have aided 55 Forever Homes, 83 Households, and 8 Neighborhoods.

Centre County Housing Authority

Contact: (814) 355-6750


The Centre County Housing Authority (CCHA) helps connect people to affordable housing options. They also advise on housing decisions, assist with lower financial class housing affordability, and provide comfortable living environments for all clients.
